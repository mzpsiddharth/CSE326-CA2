Step-by-Step Reporting Guide

Follow these two phases in order from online pre-arrival setup to physical verification at Unipolis.

Phase 1: Before Arriving on Campus

  1. Upload Documents Online: Log in to the LPU admission portal and upload scanned copies of your 10th/12th certificates and ID proof.
  2. Book Hostel & Mess: Select your residential room type and mess plan online before travel.
  3. Book Complimentary Pick-up: Reserve free campus shuttle service from Phagwara or Jalandhar Cantt railway stations.
  4. Download Reporting Slip: Save the QR-coded reporting pass on your phone.

Phase 2: On-Campus Unipolis Counters

  1. Counter 1 - QR Scan & Token: Scan your reporting pass at the Unipolis entrance gate.
  2. Counter 2 - Original Document Check: Present original certificates for physical verification.
  3. Counter 3 - Fee & Balance Clearance: Verify fee receipts and clear any pending dues.
  4. Counter 4 - Biometric & ID Photo: Capture your photo and fingerprint for your LPU ID card.
  5. Counter 5 - Hostel Key Handover: Collect your hostel allotment slip and proceed to your residential block.
