EDU-Revolution Framework

A student-centric initiative turning real-world projects, startups, and hackathons into academic credits.

The 50-50-50 Formula

50% Flexible Learning

Earn course credits through real-world projects, certifications, and internships.

50% Industry Exposure

Direct mentorship from industry practitioners and live corporate problem statements.

50% Outcome-Driven

Focus on building portfolios, patents, research papers, and student startups.

How to Apply (UMS → LMS Steps)

  1. Log in to UMS (University Management System) using your registration number.
  2. Open the LMS / EDU-Revolution dashboard from the navigation menu.
  3. Choose from the 9 nomination categories (Projects, Hackathons, Startups, Research, Patents, Social Impact, Cultural, Sports, or Certifications).
  4. Upload supporting proof and submit for faculty review.
  5. For physical assistance, visit Block 27, Room 102.
